### Installing on a Mac

1. Download **Mnemox.dmg**.
2. Open it from your Downloads folder.
3. Double-click **Mnemox** in the window that opens. It moves itself into Applications and opens. (Dragging it onto the Applications shortcut works too.)
4. The first time, macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click **Open**. Mnemox is signed and checked by Apple, so you will only see this once.

Mnemox updates itself. A new version downloads in the background, and Mnemox asks to restart when it is ready. To check now, choose **Mnemox → Check for Updates…**. On an older Mac, [mnemox.so](https://mnemox.so) in your browser does everything the app does.

### Installing on Windows (beta)

1. Download **Mnemox-Setup.exe**.
2. Open it from your Downloads folder.
3. The beta isn't signed yet, so you may see two warnings. If Edge says the file "**isn't commonly downloaded**", click **⋯** next to it, then **Keep** → **Show more** → **Keep anyway**. If Windows then shows "**Windows protected your PC**", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Mnemox installs just for you, with no admin rights needed, and opens on its own. It adds itself to the Start menu and the desktop.

Mnemox updates itself, as on a Mac. To check now, go to **Settings → About → Check for updates**. To remove it, use **Settings → Apps → Installed apps** in Windows.

## What it does

**Write anywhere, lose nothing**
- Every keystroke is saved on your device first, so you can keep writing on a train or in a meeting with no signal.
- Notes sync across your computer, phone and browser on their own. There is no sync button.
- If you edit the same note in two places, even when both were offline, the changes merge instead of asking which one to keep.

**Write the way you think**
- Headings, collapsible sections, bulleted, numbered and checklist lists, tables, quotes and code.
- Markdown shortcuts and `/` commands, so you rarely need the toolbar.
- Folders nested to any depth, tags, favourites, and tabs for several notes at once on a computer.
- Fast search across every note, plus find within a note.

**Turn notes into work** *(Pro)*
- Push a whole note, or a single line, to **Linear** or **Jira**. The issue's live status shows next to the line it came from.
- Create a note for each of today's **Google Calendar** meetings in one click.

**Share and draw**
- Share a note or a whole folder as a read-only link, with an optional expiry, and switch it off at any time.
- *(Pro)* Draw with a canvas inside any note, add images, and share links that let others edit with you live.

**Your notes stay yours**
- Export any note as Markdown, or every note at once as a zip in the same folders as your sidebar.
- Import Markdown, Word, HTML and text files, or a whole exported folder from Obsidian or Notion.
- Reminders on iPhone and Android, with a notification that opens the note.
- Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Italian, Dutch, Danish and Swedish.
